This paper explores the effect of substantial shifts in political narratives on attitudes towards migration in the US. We document a marked increase in the share of political advertising aired on TV that addresses the topic of migration, from 1% in 2012 to 12% in 2018. Exploiting the geographic segmentation of media markets and timing of the advertisements, we study the causal effect of political advertising on the volume and content of migration-related posts on Twitter.
